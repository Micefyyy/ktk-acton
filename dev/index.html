Our Mission

What is Kids Teaching Kids?

We are a student-run nonprofit that hosts free online courses throughout the year for elementary and middle school students. Our community-based approach covers English, science, history, math and more. We believe in creating a strong academic base and introducing supplemental lessons in an engaging way.

About Us

Who We Are

Kids Teaching Kids is a student-run nonprofit founded in 2020. We provide free online courses to elementary and middle school students, taught by passionate student volunteers from local high schools.

Our mission is to create a strong academic base and introduce supplemental lessons in an engaging way. Every course is designed to be fun, interactive, and accessible to all students regardless of background.

Our Story

Our History

Kids Teaching Kids started in 2020 when a group of high school students wanted to give back to their community during the pandemic. What began as a few informal tutoring sessions grew into a structured nonprofit with dozens of courses and hundreds of students.

Today, we run courses in math, science, writing, Spanish, and entrepreneurship — all taught by student volunteers who are passionate about education and community service.
