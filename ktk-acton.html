About Us

Who Is "Kids Teaching Kids"?

Around April of 2020, students began attending school online. And while working from the comfort of home had its perks, the amount of education a child received decreased. It wasn't the same.

So, a few teenagers gathered to make a small difference. Every Friday, just for an hour, they'd have two lessons for little five or six-year-olds to enjoy. Over time, the number of teachers increased, as did the number of students.

We chose the name "Kids Teaching Kids" because, well, we're not exactly professionals. But we work to achieve our goal of creating an engaging and educational learning experience for young kids. We simplify complex topics to make learning both fun and effective, ensuring every child can benefit from our resources.

Currently, we offer a variety of courses in areas like writing, biology, and math. These classes are designed to provide valuable student learning resources in an engaging format. We welcome you to experience our classes and discover if they're the right fit for your learning journey.

Our History

How It All Began

From a small Friday afternoon video call to a thriving nonprofit — here's the KTK story.

2020
Spring 2020

Founded During the Pandemic

In April 2020, as schools shifted online, a group of local 8th-grade students decided to act. They hosted simple Friday sessions for kindergarteners two lessons, a little warmth, and a lot of heart.

2021
2021 Growing

Expanding the Team & Subjects

Word spread. More student volunteers joined, enrollment climbed, and subjects expanded from basic literacy to math, science, and more.

2022
2022 Official

Becoming a Nonprofit

KTK officially incorporated as a student-run nonprofit with a formal board a President, Vice Presidents, and Marketing Director, all high school students.

Now
Today

Thriving & Expanding

Multiple courses every season Cell Biology to Advanced Math with a diverse team from ABRHS and RJ Grey. The mission stays the same: make learning fun, free, and for everyone.

TAing at KTK

A TA monitors the class, helps if technical issues arise, records lessons on request, takes attendance, and keeps class running smoothly. No preparation needed — perfect for getting started!

Additional Info for Teachers

As a teacher, you set the time and dates for your classes. KTK will advertise your course to students and families.

Classes are hosted on our platform, so you will need a slideshow or Google Doc to share on screen. Including text, pictures, and occasional videos helps keep students engaged.

Courses should have at least 4 lessons (up to around 10) around a single topic. Classes run 30 to 60 minutes, once a week. Co-teaching with another student is always welcome!

Additional Info for Chapter Heads

Chapter heads are responsible for building and managing KTK in their local area. This includes recruiting student teachers, coordinating course schedules, and making sure classes run smoothly.

You will work closely with the KTK board and have full support as you grow your chapter. No prior experience running an organization is necessary, just drive and a genuine interest in education.

If you are interested in starting a chapter in your school or community, reach out at ktkacton@gmail.com and we will walk you through the process.
